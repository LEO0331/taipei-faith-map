# Cultural review workflow / 文化審閱流程

## Present status

This procedure is prepared for future external review. **No external outreach, reviewer consent, religious-representative review or institutional endorsement is recorded for V1.4.** Current `internal-reviewed` metadata describes project checking of published sources. A prepared process, a public source link or silence from an organization must never be represented as approval.

## Prepare a review packet

For each proposed review, identify the exact organization/content IDs and the version or commit under review. Include both languages, the illustrative-scene disclaimer, every factual claim and its direct source, date precision and any competing source accounts. Keep present-day registration information separate from historical assertions and current event arrangements. Include the exact proposed UI wording and screenshots only when they are available and current.

State the requested scope plainly: factual accuracy, terminology, translation, visitor etiquette, or the representation of a particular tradition. Ask the reviewer to identify their role and the limits of what they can speak for. A knowledgeable individual may comment on wording without representing an institution or an entire religious community. Request only necessary contact information, keep any private correspondence outside published artifacts, and do not send a request without the user's authorization.

## Record and apply feedback

| Record | Required detail |
| --- | --- |
| Scope | Specific profile, tradition, event, route, milestone, story or chapter IDs and language/version. |
| Reviewer context | Role or affiliation as voluntarily supplied; whether the person is authorized to represent an organization. |
| Review dates | When the version was provided and when feedback was received. |
| Feedback | Corrections, uncertainty, disputed claims and suggested wording, linked to the individual claim. |
| Publication permission | What feedback, identity or quotations may be published; do not infer consent. |
| Editorial decision | Accepted changes, unresolved questions, reasons for retaining or withholding content and the resulting commit. |
| Limits | Topics and versions that were not reviewed; any expiry or requested future confirmation. |

Use `pending` while a factual or identity question is unresolved, or omit the specific claim. Recheck public evidence before changing wording. Keep precision labels where sources differ, and never override an address mismatch merely because a reviewer recognizes a similar name. Regenerate the content audit and run the existing validation/test/build commands after edits.

Only set `community-reviewed` after a documented review actually occurs and its relevant scope can be substantiated in the contribution record. It does not automatically extend to every paragraph, another language, later revisions, unrelated buildings or future events. Do not add an endorsement badge or imply that a community approved the application. Changes made after review may require renewed review or a return to `internal-reviewed`/`pending`, depending on what changed.

## Corrections and disputed accounts

Provide a reproducible issue or contribution containing the content ID, claim, evidence and proposed correction. Distinguish competing historical accounts from a demonstrated data-entry mistake. Narrow the text to supported common ground, label uncertainty or withhold a claim when necessary. Preserve the government's original record separately; curated corrections should not silently rewrite the generated dataset. Avoid publishing personal information from correspondence when documenting the decision.

V1.5 or subsequent work can use this packet for a small, explicitly scoped review after authorization. Reviewers should see the exact version users will read; a generic request to approve an entire religion's depiction is too broad to substantiate.

## 繁體中文

本程序已備妥供未來外部審閱使用。**V1.4沒有記錄已對外聯絡、取得審閱同意、宗教代表審查或機構背書。** `internal-reviewed` 只表示專案核對公開來源；流程文件、來源連結或對方未回覆都不能當成認可。

準備審閱資料包時，列明確切內容ID與版本／commit、雙語文案、文化示意聲明、每項主張與直接證據、日期精度及歧異資料；目前登記、歷史及當代活動安排應分開。附上使用者會看到的確切文字，畫面只有在確實存在且符合該版本時才納入。

明確說明請求範圍，例如史實、用詞、翻譯、禮儀或特定傳統的呈現。審閱者的角色、代表權限與評論限制需依其自願提供的資訊記錄；個人熟悉文化不等於可代表整個宗教或機構。只索取必要聯絡資料，私人往來不直接發布，未經使用者授權不代發審閱邀請。

審閱紀錄應包括：確切ID、語言與版本；角色及代表權限；送交及回覆日期；逐項意見與證據；可公開的回饋／身分／引文授權；採用或暫緩的編輯決定及commit；未審閱內容、版本與再確認條件。身分或事實尚未釐清時使用 `pending` 或暫不收錄該主張。地址不符不能只靠熟悉名稱便忽略。修改後重新執行內容稽核與既有驗證、測試、建置。

只有實際完成且能記錄範圍的社群審閱，才可使用 `community-reviewed`。它不自動延伸到其他段落、語言、後續改版、其他建築或未來活動，也不代表社群批准整個應用程式。審閱後修改可能需要重新確認，或依變更退回 `internal-reviewed`／`pending`。

回報修正時提供內容ID、具體主張、來源及建議，區分史料歧異與已證實的輸入錯誤；必要時縮小到共同支持的文字、標示不確定或暫不提供。政府原始紀錄維持獨立，不在人工內容修訂中默默改寫資料集，也不在編輯決策中公開不必要的私人資訊。後續版本可在獲授權後先進行小範圍、針對確切版本的外部審閱。
