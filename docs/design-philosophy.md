# 慢慢探索，重新感受。

**Explore slowly. Notice deeply.**

Taipei Faith Map offers self-paced cultural exploration that respects the visitor’s attention. A brief visit, a single chapter or a pause in the garden can be the whole experience. People of any faith or none can choose what to explore. The project is designed for cultural reading and observation. It has no clinical validation as a medical or attention-training intervention.

| Principle | Product decision |
| --- | --- |
| One Thing at a Time | The welcome leads into a place or map. Route cards preview the sequence, the selected stop receives focus and a story presents one chapter at a time. Citations stay beside the material they support. Calendar entries distinguish dated announcements from general festival references. |
| From Screen to World | Read a brief observation at each route stop, then look at the surroundings. The stop order reflects a cultural theme. Google Maps handles directions, and the site or organizer confirms visiting conditions. Current registered locations anchor map links. |
| Permission to Stop | Close a Faith Space, skip motion, leave a story or end a route with the existing controls. A map or timeline detour returns to the prior reading or walking context. The Reflection Timer remains optional. Joining and leaving the garden remain deliberate actions. |

Keyboard focus, bilingual labels, visible source links, reduced motion and readable contrast make these choices available at different screen sizes. Keep essential controls available when adjusting information density. Design decisions favor the visitor’s chosen pace over session length. Streaks, rewards, autoplay and urgency have no place in that experience.

訪客可以只看一個熱點、讀一章、走一站，或在庭院坐一會兒。路線的觀察提示引導人抬頭看現場，故事和地圖之間的返回動作保留原來的閱讀位置。這是文化探索工具，沒有臨床驗證的醫療或專注力訓練用途。
